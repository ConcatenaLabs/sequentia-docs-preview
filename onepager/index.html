Send and hold bitcoin, dollars, gold and stocks with one Bitcoin address and one seed. Pay the fee in whatever you are sending.

Pay fees in any asset you holdSending dollars? Pay the fee in dollars. Sequentia is the first free market for transaction fees: no coin you have to buy first.
It is an actual Bitcoin walletNo new address format, no new seed. Your keys hold your bitcoin and every other asset in one place.

Two reasons to care

A better Bitcoin wallet. And the end of gas tokens.

Sequentia is two things at once. Start from whichever matters to you.

For everyone who holds bitcoin

The Bitcoin wallet we dreamed of ten years ago

Bitcoin sent in seconds and for free with Ark, Lightning to pay anyone outside, and no channels to manage. Then every other asset on the same address.

For stablecoins and finance

The end of gas tokens

To move $100 of USDT on Ethereum or Tron you first buy ETH or TRX. On Sequentia you pay the fee in the dollars you are sending.

Try it now

  1. Grab free test assets. Open the web wallet, press the faucet buttons and fill it with test bitcoin, dollars, gold or oil.
  2. Pay a friend in dollars, fee in dollars. Or in any other asset in your wallet. Block producers accept any fee that has a market price.
  3. Swap with bitcoin. Trade a test asset for BTC: no exchange account, no custodian, no peg.

Sequentia runs as a public testnet, anchored to Bitcoin testnet4. There is no mainnet yet, and testnet coins and assets have no value.
