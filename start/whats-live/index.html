What is live today

Live on testnet

Sequentia is not a plan. The public testnet has produced blocks continuously since its re-genesis on 5 July 2026, anchored to Bitcoin testnet4, and a full set of applications runs on it. All of them are open source under github.com/ConcatenaLabs.

Three labels are used on this page:

  • Live running on the public testnet and usable now;
  • Experimental running, but depending on a provisional piece, or said to be experimental in the application itself;
  • Proof of concept a working demonstration, with parts that are simulated or centralised and stated as such.

Everything is testnet software. There is no mainnet, and nothing here should be used with anything of value. Is it up right now? The status page checks every service once a minute.

SeqognitoDesktop mixing wallet: CoinJoin over confidential transactions, everything over Tor.Experimentalseqognito

Which one to pick: Get a wallet.

Trading

What it isStateCode
SeqDEXOrder-book exchange with no custody. Markets, charts, market and limit orders; settles asset for asset on-chain and asset for real BTC across chains.Liveseqdex, seqdex-web
Lightning swapsInstant asset-for-BTC trades over Lightning. Depends on a hosted demo backend today.Experimentalseqln
Payment-proof swapsSell an asset for a plain Bitcoin payment, verified by every node. Written, not yet activated.Comingspecification

How trades settle: Swaps and SeqDEX.

Second layers

What it isStateCode
Bitcoin on ArkThe web wallet holds your bitcoin on a Bitcoin ark (Bark, by Second): paid in seconds between wallets, and over Lightning to anyone else.Livesequentia-web-wallet
ArcaArk for Sequentia assets: instant off-chain payments in any asset, with an exit to the chain nobody can refuse.Experimentalarca
SeqLNLightning for Sequentia: channels and payments in any issued asset, from the same binary that runs Bitcoin Lightning.Experimentalseqln

Assets and issuance

What it isStateCode
FaucetFree test assets to any address: tSEQ, a dollar and a euro stablecoin, tokenised gold, silver and oil.Livesequentia-faucet
Asset registryMaps asset ids to verified names, tickers and issuer domains for every wallet and the explorer.Livesequentia-registry
OpenAMPIssuer-governed restricted assets: every transfer co-signed by the issuer’s policy server. An open-source equivalent of Blockstream’s AMP2.Liveopenamp
OpenDAMPRegulated assets enforced by the network itself: covenants police the policy, with no server in a transfer’s path.Experimentalopendamp
SeqPalTokenisation as a service: structure, issue and service a security token. The issuance is real; identity checks and legal steps are simulated.Proof of conceptSeqPal

Choosing between them: For issuers.
